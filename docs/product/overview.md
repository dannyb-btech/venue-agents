# Product overview

**What:** AI integration consulting for hospitality, delivered as a platform: role-based Claude agents
connected to a venue's existing stack that take over restaurant admin and reduce management load.
Agents draft; managers approve in one inbox.

**Pilot:** OR (testimonial and case study, terms to agree). Then productise for client two and beyond.

**OR's stack:** Google Workspace (Gmail, Drive), Webflow, SevenRooms, Toast, Meta, Tanda, Xero, Stripe, ANZ.

## First three agents

- **Events Manager** — drafts event-enquiry replies; finds past clients and drafts tailored outreach.
- **Wine Buyer** — reviews sales, drafts orders, writes staff tasting notes for under-performers.
- **Office Manager** — reviews payroll and labour costs, drafts rosters to labour-% targets.

Later: Admin Assistant (credit applications, forms), Marketing (posts, EDMs, website updates), more.

## Success measures for the pilot

- Three agents live at OR within 12 weeks.
- Measured hours saved per week (tracked in-app).
- Draft acceptance rate (approved without edits) rising week on week.
- OR signs off the case study.
